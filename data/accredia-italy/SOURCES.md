# ACCREDIA (Italy)

Source: ACCREDIA (Ente Italiano di Accreditamento) database of accredited certification bodies, ISO/IEC 17021-1 management systems, https://services.accredia.it/ppsearch/accredia_orgmask.jsp (search by standard ISO/IEC 17021-1, then each body's detail page). Scraped with `../database/italy-claude/database/italy/scrape_it_accredia.py` (`accredia_raw.json`), converted with `../database/italy-claude/prepare_certifind.py`; notes, skipped schemes and pruned rows are in `accredia_review.md` there. Not yet verified by a person, so `verified_at` is empty. `source_url` and `proof_url` are the body's register detail page. The accreditation body row comes from `V2__reference_data.sql`; no new migration is needed.

Extraction rules:
- One ACCREDIA certificate number per body (e.g. 00005); all schemes share its issue and expiry dates. Suffixed rows use that number.
- Accreditation standard per scheme as in the other datasets: ISO/IEC 27001/27701 → ISO/IEC 27006 (" (27006)"), ISO 50001 → ISO 50003 (" (50003)"), ISO 22000/FSSC 22000 → ISO 22003-1 (" (22000)"), ISO/IEC 20000-1 → ISO/IEC 20000-6 (" (20000)"), ISO/IEC 42001 → ISO/IEC 42006 (" (42001)"), ISO 22301 → ISO/IEC 17021-6 (" (22301)"), ISO 37001 → ISO/IEC 17021-9 (" (37001)"), ISO 55001 → ISO/IEC 17021-5 (" (55001)"), ISO 39001 → ISO/IEC 17021-7 (" (39001)"), ISO 20121 → ISO/IEC 17021-4 (" (20121)"), EN 9100 (taken as AS 9100) → AS 9104-1 (" (9104)"); everything else (ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, 13485, 3834, 21001, 28000, 37301, 19443, 21401, 29001, 56001, 7101) under ISO/IEC 17021-1.
- EA codes: ISO 9001 / 14001 / 45001 use the register's IAF sectors (ranges such as "01-07b" expanded; "*" = sector accredited with a restriction, kept). ISO 22000 categories (ISO 22003-1), ISO 50001 technical areas (ISO 50003, numbered 1.1-6.0 by ACCREDIA) and ISO 13485 technical areas (IAF MD 8 1.x, sub-codes such as 1.7.A reduced to the main area) are classified to EA with the ESYD tables. ISO 22301 sector letters and ISO/IEC 20000-1 NACE codes are not classified; those scopes, and every scheme without a code list, are recorded for EA 1-39 with a note.
- Country from the end of the register address (Italy, Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Israel, India, Kosovo). Phones rewritten to international form (+39 keeps the leading 0; second numbers dropped); only the first email is kept.
- Bodies already in another dataset reuse that body row: URS Certification Ltd. (`nabcb-india`); IAS Register AG (`sas-switzerland`); IGQ - Istituto Italiano di Garanzia della Qualità (`anab-usa`); RINA Services SPA (`anab-usa`). Perry Johnson Registrars, Inc. (Italian address, certificate 00277) is a separate Italy row from the United States body in other datasets.
- Dropped - register detail without accreditation date or address (not scraped): ITALCERTIFER S.p.A. (ISO 55001); AFNOR ITALIA S.r.l. (ISO 37001); VALORITALIA S.r.l. (ISO 21401).
- Dropped - the scrape only says "other MS schemes" without naming them: CEC - CONSORZIO EUROPEO CERTIFICAZIONE S.c.ar.l. (00480); VERICERT S.r.l. (01079); INAIL - Dipartimento Innovazioni Tecnologiche (Organismo Notificato) (00991); PT Bureau Veritas Indonesia (01534); EUCER S.r.l. (01336); I.C.M. Institut de Certification Mediterraneen S.a.r.l. (02313); SA CERTIFICATION S.r.l. (01408); AREAS Certificazioni S.r.l. (01947); AENOR ITALIA S.R.L. (02115); Italia ESG S.r.l. (02241); CISE - Centro per l'Innovazione e lo Sviluppo Economico (02249); Politiche Attive Lazio CDL S.r.l. (02387); PAL Certificazioni S.r.l. (02418); ASACERT ASSESSMENT AND CERTIFICATION S.P.A. SOCIETA' BENEFIT (00373); CSI S.p.A. (00009); SIDEIUS S.r.l. (00055).
- Dropped - no ISO/IEC 17021-1 scope: CCPB S.r.l. (ISO 22000 only), QUINEL Limited and MSECB Management Systems Inc. (ISO/IEC 27001 only). IQUA NQA ITALIA has only the schemes below.
- Not included: EN 9110, EN 9120, PAS 24000 - not in the standards table; UNI/PdR 74, UNI 11337-8 - Italian national schemes.
