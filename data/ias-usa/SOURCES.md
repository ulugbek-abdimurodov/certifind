# IAS (United States)

Source: IAS (International Accreditation Service) register of accredited Management Systems Certification Bodies (ISO/IEC 17021-1), https://www.iasonline.org/search-accredited-organizations-2/, with each body's certificate and scope of accreditation PDF. Scraped with `../database/usa/ias_certifind/database/usa/scrape_us_ias.py` (`raw/ias_raw.txt`), converted with `../database/usa/ias_certifind/prepare_certifind.py`; notes, skipped schemes and pruned rows are in `ias_review.md` there. Not yet verified by a person, so `verified_at` is empty. `source_url` is the register search page; `proof_url` is the certificate PDF.

Extraction rules:
- One IAS certificate is one accreditation, certificate number `MSCB-<n>`. `issue_date` is the initial accreditation date (the certificate's effective date where none is printed), `expiry_date` the register expiry.
- Accreditation standard per scheme as in the other datasets: ISO/IEC 27001/27701 → ISO/IEC 27006 (" (27006)"), ISO 50001 → ISO 50003 (" (50003)"), ISO 22000/FSSC 22000 → ISO 22003-1 (" (22000)"), ISO/IEC 20000-1 → ISO/IEC 20000-6 (" (20000)"), ISO/IEC 42001 → ISO/IEC 42006 (" (42001)"), ISO 22301 → ISO/IEC 17021-6 (" (22301)"), ISO 37001 → ISO/IEC 17021-9 (" (37001)"), ISO 55001 → ISO/IEC 17021-5 (" (55001)"), ISO 39001 → ISO/IEC 17021-7 (" (39001)"), ISO 20121 → ISO/IEC 17021-4 (" (20121)"); everything else (ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, 13485, 37301, 41001, 21001, 29993) under ISO/IEC 17021-1.
- EA codes: ISO 9001 / 14001 / 45001 use the IAF codes in the scope. ISO 13485 IAF MD 8 tables A1.1-A1.7 and ISO 22000 / FSSC 22000 food chain categories (ISO 22003-1) are classified to EA with the ESYD tables. All other schemes print "Certification Sectors – N/A" and are recorded for EA 1-39 with a note.
- Country from the register; two contradict the certificate address and follow the address: QRC Assurance and Solutions Inc. (listed IN, address USA) and Zhongci International Certification (listed SA, address China). Addresses come from the PDF text; some have broken words (e.g. "G EORGIA") and are kept as published. IAS publishes no phone or email.
- Suspended bodies (Adriacert, China Standards Institute Guolian, FAP Certification (Shanghai), FQC First Quality Certification) are dropped.
