# DPA (Albania)

Source: DPA accreditation certificates and annexes, https://www.dpa.gov.al.
Transcribed by hand from the annex PDFs (CS 006 Legal Cert, CS 007 AXE Register,
CS 008–CS 019). Not yet verified against current DPA records: `verified_at` is empty.

Transcription rules:

- Sector lists come from the "Nr. IAF" column of each annex.
- Lettered categories are classified to EA codes by sector: ISO 50001 A–H,
  ISO 22000 A–K, ISO 13485 device categories. ISO 22301 covers all eight
  categories, so it is recorded as `ALL`.
- Annexes that say "all IAF codes" (Legal Cert 27006 and 37001) or give no sector
  list (AXE 27001, 27701, 27017, 27018, 20000-1, 39001, 20121, 37001) are `ALL`.
- AXE 9001 / 14001 / 45001 annexes list EA 1–39 except 11 (`1-10;12-39`).
- EA 7 is limited to paper products in some annexes; EA 17 is split (17a/17b) in the
  R.C.A 14001/45001 annexes. Both are recorded in `scope_note`.
- `proof_url` is the DPA home page; replace it with the certificate's own URL when known.
