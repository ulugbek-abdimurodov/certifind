# BATA (Bosnia and Herzegovina) - ISO/IEC 17065

Source: Institute for Accreditation of Bosnia and Herzegovina (BATA), "List of accredited conformity assessment
bodies on 2026-10-20" (`List-of-accredited-bodies.pdf`, English), section D "Product certification bodies"
(6 bodies), http://bata.gov.ba/. Transcribed by hand; not yet verified by a person, so `verified_at` is empty.
The annex and certificate links in the PDF were not followed; `proof_url` is the BATA home page.

Rules:

- Certificate number = BATA accreditation number (CP-xx-xx); `issue_date` = date of granting; the list gives no
  expiry date.
- One line per item of the "Range" column; `scheme_text` = the "Scope" wording. No catalogue scheme (national
  BAS product certification); sector construction or agriculture.
- IRC d.o.o. (CP-35-02): entire scope suspended from 2026-10-20 (decision 05-44-4-160-3/26): accreditation and
  line status SUSPENDED.
- Phones made international (+387, leading 0 dropped); fax numbers left out. Sections A-C and E (laboratories,
  ISO/IEC 17025 / 15189, and inspection bodies, ISO/IEC 17020) are not product certification and are not
  imported.
