# TÜRKAK (Turkey)

Source: TÜRKAK's list of Management Systems Certification bodies (type `YS`),
https://asist.turkak.org.tr/en/accreditation/accreditationagencysearch?institutionType=YS,
including suspended and withdrawn bodies. Contacts, status and dates come from the TÜRKAK
API; scopes come from the signed certificate annex: the English PDF, or the Turkish one
when no English certificate is published. `proof_url` and `source_url` are each body's
TÜRKAK certificate page. Not yet verified by a person, so `verified_at` is empty.

These CSVs are generated by `turkak_scraper.py` and `prepare_certifind.py` (in
`database/turkak-scraper`, **outside this repository**):

```
python turkak_scraper.py
python prepare_certifind.py --download-tr --out <certifind>/data/turkak-turkey
```

`prepare_certifind.py` also writes `turkak_data/REVIEW.md`, which lists every value it
normalised, every category it mapped to EA codes, and every annex it could not convert.

Extraction rules:

- TÜRKAK puts all schemes on one certificate (`AB-xxxx-YS`). Each extra accreditation
  standard gets its own row with a suffix, as in the RENAR and DPA datasets:
  `(27006)` for 27001/27701, `(50003)`, `(22000)` for ISO 22000 and FSSC 22000 (ISO 22003-1),
  `(20000)` for 20000-1 (ISO/IEC 20000-6), `(42001)` for 42001 (ISO/IEC 42006).
  ISO 13485 and ISO 22301 stay on the 17021-1 row, as the annexes list them.
- `issue_date` is TÜRKAK's initial accreditation date and `expiry_date` its validity date.
  Every row of a certificate shares them.
- `status` comes from the TÜRKAK status: `Active…` = ACTIVE, `Suspend…` = SUSPENDED,
  `Accreditation Withdrawn…` = WITHDRAWN; suspended and withdrawn bodies are then removed
  (see the last rule). Partial withdrawals and suspensions ("… Some
  Scopes") are not modelled; the annex is taken to list the scopes still held.
- Sector lists come from the "EA Code" / "EA Kodu" column of each annex.
- ISO 50001 technical areas, ISO 22000 / FSSC 22000 categories and ISO 13485 technical
  areas are classified to EA codes with the tables in `prepare_certifind.py`, copied from
  how the RENAR and DPA datasets classified them, and listed in `scope_note`.
  **This mapping is a draft and needs review.**
- Annexes with no sector list (27001, 27701, 20000-1, 22301, 42001) are `ALL`.
- Not imported: EU directive / notified-body annexes, AS 9100 / 9110 / 9120 (aerospace),
  ISO 46001, and branch offices.
- Website without a scheme gets `https://`; Turkish phone numbers missing `+90` get it.
- Country is Turkey, except bodies whose city is Bakü (Azerbaijan) or Taşkent (Uzbekistan).
- 16 bodies have no scopes: 15 with no certificate published (14 of them suspended or
  withdrawn) and AB-0181-YS, whose only annex is an EU directive.
- Only bodies that currently hold a valid accreditation are kept: withdrawn, expired (including an active-listed row whose valid-to date has passed) and suspended bodies and rows are removed by `database/prune_inactive.py`, which the prepare script runs after writing the CSVs.
